About Me

Design is how I earn a reader's or a customer's first impression.

A closer look at how I got here, and how I think about every project I take on.

My Story

I started designing book covers because I was tired of seeing good books get overlooked for bad ones. Four years and 60-plus covers later, that's still what pulls me into every project: making sure the work underneath a cover gets a fair shot at being noticed.

Since then my client list has grown past authors into founders, agencies and creators who all share the same problem. Their work is strong, but their visuals aren't saying that yet.

Design Philosophy

I believe good design is a business decision before it's an aesthetic one. A cover, a logo or a thumbnail has one job in the first two seconds someone sees it: earn enough trust to keep looking. Everything I build is judged against that standard first, style second.

  • 01
    Clarity over decorationIf an element doesn't help someone understand or trust the work faster, it doesn't belong.
  • 02
    Category awarenessI study what's already succeeding in your space before I suggest breaking from it.
  • 03
    Honest feedbackI'll tell you when a request will hurt the outcome, and offer what will work instead.

Why Clients Come Back

Clients return because I treat their project like it's the only one on my desk. Clear communication, realistic timelines and a genuine opinion, not just execution of instructions, are what most freelance experiences are missing.
